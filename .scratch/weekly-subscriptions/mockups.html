Weekly subscriptions, built for meal kits

A third Billing Cycle where the unit of decision is the week. The user plans deliveries by toggling individual weeks between Deliver and Skip — at creation, from the subscription, and straight from Upcoming — without touching the pause/resume lifecycle machinery.

Backend

How it lands in the data model

The core insight: a skipped week is not a pause. Pausing is a lifecycle state with events and history semantics; skipping is a per-occurrence plan. One new table plus one new cycle value covers the whole feature — projections stay pure, history stays append-only.

Lifecycle of a skipped week

1 · Planned

User taps Skip. A Skipped Week row is stored for that projected billing date. Upcoming hides or ghosts it; totals drop.

2 · Changed mind

Tapping Deliver (or Resume on the ghost row) deletes the row. The projection instantly includes the week again. Nothing else to reconcile.

3 · Date passes

On next initialize, the skipped date materializes as a Billing Occurrence with a Generated Charge Exception ("Skipped week") linked to the Skipped Week record — mirroring how cancellation resolves unbilled history (ADR-0003, ADR-0004).

4 · History

The exception shows in Subscription Activity as "Not charged · Skipped week." Recorded Spend is untouched; Action Needed never nags about a week you chose to skip.

Reminders reuse the existing pipeline: the Billing Reminder targets the next unskipped occurrence, and any toggle flags reminder reconciliation exactly like pause/resume does today. Duplicate detection extends unchanged — same name, amount, cycle, and a next date within three days.

To confirm

Open decisions

Monthly Commitment for weekly subscriptions

Normalize at 52 ⁄ 12 (≈ 4.33 weeks ⁄ month) regardless of skips, keeping it a stable ceiling like today's definition — or count planned deliveries, which makes it jump week to week. Recommend 52 ⁄ 12; skips already show up in "Next 30 Days."

Default for "Show skipped weeks in Upcoming"

On keeps the resume affordance discoverable where the user already lives; off keeps Upcoming strictly "money leaving." Recommend on by default for weekly subs, per-subscription override at creation and in edit.

Can a past week's skip be undone?

Deleting the Skipped Week after materialization would cascade its Generated Charge Exception and surface the week in Action Needed. Recommend no — correct history via the Billing Occurrence (record a payment over the exception) instead of editing the plan retroactively.

Pause still applies to weekly subscriptions

Skipping covers week-to-week choices; a long vacation or account freeze is still a proper Pause with its Lifecycle Event. The Week Planner hides while paused. No change proposed — flagging so the two mechanisms stay distinct in the docs.
