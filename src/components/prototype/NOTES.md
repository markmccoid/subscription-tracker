# Dashboard prototype verdict

**Question:** Which Dashboard information hierarchy makes upcoming charges feel most actionable without becoming financially noisy?

- **A — Chronological calm:** Totals first, then charges grouped by time horizon.
- **B — Decision first:** The most urgent trial or price decision leads, with upcoming charges secondary.
- **C — Compact ledger:** Calendar and dense expected-charge rows optimize scanning.

**Verdict:** A — Chronological calm. Keep its totals-first, time-grouped structure. Give the Next 7 Days section a warm amber treatment, and use red with a Review action only for an individual Subscription whose renewal is fewer than three days away.
